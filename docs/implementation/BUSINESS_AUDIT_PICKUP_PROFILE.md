# Capability-owned pickup business audit profile

**Product:** v0.0.1. **Owner decision:** accepted on 2026-10-10 through [OPS-013's proposal](../review/OPS_013_DECISION_PROPOSAL.md). **Qualification:** locally `PRODUCTION_HONEST`, `BLOCKED = none` for this selected profile, with [exact evidence and recovery](../review/COM_016_OPS_013_IMPLEMENTATION_RECEIPT.md). The underlying [Fulfillment scope](ORDER_SERVICE_FULFILLMENT.md) retains its prior qualification.

The selected transition is successful operator-recorded pickup in the accepted single-line in-house non-stock program Order case. Reuse Fulfillment's existing work source, immutable event journal, activity links, caller-scoped command receipts and protected history. No second ledger, audit service, generic event store or new runtime process is introduced. Persisted success is an accepted recorded business effect, not proof of physical delivery, customer signature, payment or legal receipt; failed/denied attempts are outside this profile.

Minimum evidence is tenant/Order/line and frozen source revision, actor, action `Handoff`, accepted outcome, event/fulfillment revision, authorization revision, server UTC recorded time and durable receipt/event identity for correlation and replay. Effect, journal/activity evidence and receipt commit together; a required evidence-write failure cannot produce a successful unaudited pickup. Same-key replay returns the retained outcome without another fact. PostgreSQL runtime grants/guards prohibit updates/deletions of retained events and receipts.

Retain the selected journal, receipts and their necessary linked source/activity facts indefinitely. There is no automatic cleanup or runtime maintenance authority. Corrections append linked facts under the existing recent initial-Owner plus correction-right contract. Database/schema owners are not runtime maintenance authorities. Future retention, legal-hold management or privileged maintenance needs a separate decision and requalification; no tamper-evident, legal-forensics or compliance claim is made.

History requires current active tenant membership and `orders.fulfillment.view`, uses the existing bounded revision-ordered pages, and performs no mutation. Recipient labels and reasons already belong to protected Fulfillment history; this accepted profile retains that access/privacy boundary and adds no broader read projection. Operational logs/telemetry never contain those values, credentials or raw bodies. Retained evidence is PostgreSQL authority, separate from operational telemetry. Current CoreApi has no OTLP exporter; a native host with logging providers removed must still write pickup and read its history after restart, without adding a telemetry implementation merely to disable one.

Recurring guards include pickup event/receipt write-failure rollback, direct restricted-role UPDATE/DELETE denial, same-key recovery/replay, bounded keyset pages under a newer event, actual provisioning-grant checks, current membership/viewer denial and the real PostgreSQL/OpenFGA/CoreApi restart journey. Normal repository verification and focused CoreApi/DbMigrator graphs must pass on the integrated source before the profile is qualified. Requalify on effect/receipt/schema/grant/version changes, access/privacy/pagination changes, retention or maintenance changes, or telemetry coupling/exporter introduction. General audit of other commercial transitions and ADM-023 administrative reads remain outside this profile.
