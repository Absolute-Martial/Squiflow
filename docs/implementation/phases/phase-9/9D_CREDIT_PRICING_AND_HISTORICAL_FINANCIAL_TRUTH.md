# Phase 9D — Credit, Pricing, and Historical Financial Truth

## Credit

Current shared exposure/credit authority is server-required where stale data could permit unsafe overspend. Offline snapshots may inform UX but do not override current central authority.

## Pricing

Support only accepted price sources needed by real flows: defaults, organization/program/wholesale, negotiated quotation, Owner-authorized final price, outsourced-production resale price.

Owner/manual final pricing must be permission/rule constrained and audited where material.

## Money/time rules

Close exact rounding/tax/timezone behavior before the implemented financial flow depends on it. Do not invent multi-currency/FX accounting without a requirement.

## Historical truth

Issued quotations/invoices/payments retain applied currency/value/configuration/revision evidence so later settings do not silently recompute them.

## Incremental development

Pricing/quotation/credit concepts may exist before Phase 9. The phase qualifies the protected current-authority and historical-financial semantics required once those concepts can affect authoritative shared exposure or posted financial truth.

## Exit gate

Protected financial decisions use current authority while issued history remains explainable after configuration/permission changes.
