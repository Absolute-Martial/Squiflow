# COM-012 / COM-013: proposed Order approval and admission contract

**Status: accepted by the owner on 2026-10-09.** Baseline: `f32576f7d86b2166e381a10b80df61865addf240`, product `v0.0.1`. No approval runtime, permission, policy or persistence contract is introduced by this document. Read the complete [COM-012 task](../development-tasks/phase-02-commercial-backend/COM-012-approval-decisions-and-human-continuation.md), [COM-013 task](../development-tasks/phase-02-commercial-backend/COM-013-current-policy-admission-for-committed-orders.md), [workflow semantics](../workflow/WORKFLOW_DESIGN.md) and [current program-reference policy](../implementation/ORDER_PROGRAM_REFERENCE_POLICY.md).

## One business choice to accept

Require an internal approval of the exact draft of a program-attributed Order before commitment when its pinned tenant policy requires approval. This is approval of recorded commercial content and supplied reference, not verification of a customer's instruction, payment, credit, stock, artwork or legal agreement. Customer acceptance of a quotation remains the separate COM-010 fact.

For Owner plus Staff: Staff submits a ready program Order and assigns an authorized Owner. The Owner finds it in the protected pending-work list and approves or rejects it. If the Owner submits instead, an independently authorized Staff member can decide. If the designated person cannot act, the current Owner can explicitly reassign the request or cancel the pending request if no eligible alternate exists. Nobody acting leaves it pending; no timeout approves anything.

## Recommended rules

| Choice | Proposed contract |
|---|---|
| Applicability | Add the typed Boolean `RequireApprovalForProgramOrders`, default `false`. When `true`, every Order with program attribution needs the approved exact subject before commitment. Non-program Orders remain outside this approval requirement. No amount threshold, discount, customer portal or configurable graph. |
| Publication and existing work | Reuse the separate tenant policy view/edit/publish and private profile publish/activate authorities. The new `tenant-profile/v2` snapshot retains the reference setting and an explicitly versioned approval setting; published policy facts retain their own supported schema meaning. Existing `profiles.policy.edit/publish` delegates are explicitly authorized to edit/publish this added setting through the same typed-policy boundary. Existing supported v1 profiles/pins retain their established reference policy and have no approval requirement. New profiles affect new creation/conversion only; no automatic migration or rewriting of older Orders or receipts. Unprofiled drafts remain fail-closed until the existing authorized explicit baseline assignment; absence never means optional policy. The optional legacy baseline remains optional for both requirements. |
| Approval subject | The subject binds the tenant and Order, exact draft revision, full priced content/attribution/quotation origin, supplied reference and immutable profile/policy revisions. Submission requires a compatible, ready draft, including a required reference when applicable. An approval never edits or commits the Order. |
| Normal approver | One named active same-tenant account with independently granted approval-view, approval-decision and Order-view authority. Owner title grants no business permission. Normal approval is forbidden for the requester and for the actor who last created, revised or reference-edited that exact Order revision. The recorded provenance must be valid; missing/contradictory provenance fails closed. |
| Independent rights | Introduce ordinary compiled/delegable `orders.approvals.submit`, `orders.approvals.view`, `orders.approvals.decide`, and `orders.approvals.recover` through the current grant/custom-role protocol. Submission and normal decision also require `orders.approvals.view` and `orders.view`; recovery discovery requires approval-view independently of recovery permission. Approval rights never grant price changes, Order editing or commitment. The recovery command additionally requires the current initial Owner and the existing recent Tenant Web authentication checks. No existing Owner/Staff grant is expanded automatically. These independent IDs support distinct grants but do not prohibit combining rights in one role; the domain self-approval check applies even then. |
| Rejection and retry | Approve or reject is terminal for that request. Rejection requires a reason and does not abandon the Order. An authorized submitter may create another request, including for unchanged content after addressing the rejection. Only one pending request is allowed per Order. A current authorized submitter may explicitly replace a stale pending request by supplying its expected request revision; submission atomically marks the old request Superseded and creates a new request for the expected current Order revision. A current pending request cannot be silently replaced. After terminal rejection or cancellation, submission creates a separate request without rewriting the earlier decision. |
| Reassignment and cancellation | The current Owner with recovery authority may reassign a pending request to a currently eligible independent account, with a reason and expected request revision. The requester may cancel their own pending request under submit authority; the current Owner may cancel under recovery authority. Cancellation does not abandon or commit the Order. |
| Unavailable-actor recovery | The current Owner with separately granted recovery authority and recent Tenant Web authentication may explicitly reassign or cancel, with a required reason. If no independent eligible account exists, keep the request pending with an actionable reason or cancel it; restore membership/grants through their existing protected administration paths before resubmitting. Recovery never approves the Owner's own work. If the current Owner is also unavailable, requester cancellation or normal protected identity/membership/Owner recovery is required; uninterrupted progress without an eligible actor is not claimed. No automatic approval, silent reassignment, emergency approval override, or Platform Admin business approval. |
| No-action and deadline | Pending work remains durable and discoverable until an explicit decision, cancellation or replacement. There is no automatic expiry, scheduler, escalation timer or external notification in this slice. Suspension/revocation makes normal decision unavailable; Owner recovery remains separately checked. |
| Subject changes | Any draft revision, including a reference edit, invalidates an earlier approval for admission. A pending stale request remains visible with its stale reason and recovery actions; it cannot be approved for the new content. A fresh request must bind the current revision. Policy activation alone does not invalidate an existing pinned subject. |
| Admission decision point | COM-013 checks the exact approved subject under the same Order transaction/lock as commitment. It preserves current actor authorization, pinned-profile/reference and current commercial-fact checks. Missing/rejected/stale approval denies commitment without transition or successful receipt effects. Approval is not permission to commit. |
| Permission changes after a decision | Membership/decision authority is checked when a decision is made and before replay of that decision command. A later suspension/revocation preserves the historical approval fact; it does not retroactively revoke approval of unchanged content. Commitment checks the committing actor's current authority and all current applicable admission facts. An explicit approval-revocation operation is not introduced. |

## Durable continuation, disclosure and concurrency

Orders owns the request, immutable subject, assignment/decision history and semantic receipts within its existing host-neutral capability and PostgreSQL adapter. No generic workflow service, new process, database, queue, in-memory durable substitute or new vendor dependency is needed.

Submission requires the expected Order revision. Each request has its own positive revision, distinct from Order revision and TenantAuthorizationRevision; assignment/state changes advance it and decisions, reassignment, cancellation and superseding require its expected value. States are Pending, Approved, Rejected, Canceled and Superseded; reassignment remains Pending and terminal states cannot be rewritten. Every mutation uses current request authority, an expected Order/request revision as applicable, caller-scoped idempotency and immutable actor/time/authorization evidence. Approve/reject/reassign/cancel races have one winner; losing attempts return a conflict. Submission, subject revision, decision and commitment coordinate through the Order lock so evidence cannot describe mixed revisions. A lost response replays the original semantic receipt only after current authority is rechecked. Changing the meaning of an idempotency key conflicts; observed authorization revision is evidence rather than new business intent.

The protected, bounded pending-work API is the first in-product continuation surface; no Web/Workstation UI is claimed. A caller with approval-view authority sees only requests they submitted, requests assigned to them, or Owner recovery work they are currently authorized to access. It includes IDs, state, revision, assignment and actionable stale/unavailable reasons. Prices, lines, customer reference text and decision reasons are excluded from inbox summaries and ordinary diagnostics; protected full subject/history access additionally requires `orders.view`. Queries do not secretly mutate a stale request. Pending assigned work remains discoverable even if the assignee's decision right is removed, with decision unavailable; Owner recovery discovery prevents loss of the work itself.

Reasons are outer-trimmed, case-preserving text of 1–512 Unicode scalar values; Unicode controls and malformed UTF-16 are rejected. Reason text is historical business evidence, not diagnostic content. Retained subject/request/receipt versions are validated, unknown or contradictory historical authority fails closed, and cross-tenant request/target IDs cannot authorize access.

## Delivery boundary and proof

Implement COM-012 records/continuation and the minimal COM-013 consumer together. A required-approval policy must not become publishable/activatable while direct commitment can bypass its check. Default and supported older work keeps its established admission behavior. The current quoted-Order protection and original conversion replay snapshot remain intact. Existing COM-008 reasoned overrides/beyond-policy authority are not replaced by approval, and current-price incompatibility still requires an explicit permitted revision.

The dependency is the existing authorization administration implementation, not a claim that its separate gate-owner acceptance is complete. Verify its exact catalog/model/reconciliation path before extending it. The [COM-011 receipt](COM_011_IMPLEMENTATION_RECEIPT.md) supplies the profile/reference prerequisite evidence. Existing live storage/ZITADEL and other gate-owner blockers remain separate.

Prove approval admission and recovery without automatic or self-approval, exact-revision invalidation, no normal self-approval, independent rights, fresh account/membership/Owner checks, confidential inbox filters, same-key replay, all decision/reassignment/commit races, retained v1 compatibility, cancellation/connection-loss rollback, restart discovery, and quote price/origin/conversion preservation using the real PostgreSQL/OpenFGA/CoreApi boundaries. Run the unchanged normal repository gate and independent affected host build/publish checks. Declare the introduced scope `BLOCKED` until those checks pass; no remote CI, deployment, coverage or full-product qualification is implied.
